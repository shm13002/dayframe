Problem 3. Eigenvalues and diagonalization

Due Thursday · Show all work

Let A be the matrix below. Determine whether A is diagonalizable over ℝ. If it is, find an invertible matrix P and a diagonal matrix D such that A = PDP−1.

A = [ 4   1 ;   2   3 ]

(a) Compute the characteristic polynomial det(A − λI).

(b) Find each eigenvalue and a basis for its eigenspace.

(c) Conclude, and give P and D.

Hint from lecture: an n×n matrix is diagonalizable exactly when the eigenspace dimensions add up to n.
